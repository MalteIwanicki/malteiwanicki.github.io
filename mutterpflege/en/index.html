Costs and insurance

Postpartum family support may be covered in certain situations

The exact requirements depend on the situation, health insurer, and medical or midwife assessment. These steps are a general orientation.

  1. Clarify the need: What support is needed after birth, illness, or a difficult family situation?
  2. Talk to your doctor or midwife and ask whether a certificate for household help or family support may be possible.
  3. Contact your health insurance provider and ask which documents, hours, and deadlines apply.
  4. Contact Robyn on WhatsApp so availability and support at home can be discussed.

FAQ

Common questions before the first message

What does a Mütterpflegerin do?

A Mütterpflegerin supports families around pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and stressful family situations. Robyn's focus is loving baby care and practical support at home.

Can health insurance cover the costs?

Depending on the situation, this may be possible through household help or family support. It is important to speak with your health insurer, doctor, or midwife early.

When should I contact Robyn?

As early as possible once you know support at home may be needed. Short-notice requests can also be discussed via WhatsApp.

Does Robyn speak German and English?

Yes. Robyn is a native English speaker and fluent in German. Families can contact her in either language.

Which areas in Ostalbkreis are possible?

Robyn supports families in Ostalbkreis. Exact distance, availability, and scheduling are clarified personally after your request.

Is this the same as midwife care?

No. A midwife provides medical and birth-related care. Mütterpflege complements this with practical relief, baby care, and everyday support.

Can Robyn help with twins or siblings?

This can be discussed depending on the situation and availability. A short description of your family situation helps in the first message.
